About the project

One workspace for your entire student life.

Student Toolkit brings together the small utilities every student ends up needing — a grade calculator, an attendance tracker, a focus timer, a task list, a motivation button, and a notepad — into one fast, distraction-free page.

No sign-ups, no ads, no backend. Everything runs directly in your browser, and your data — tasks and notes — stays saved on your own device.

  • Works fully offline after first load
  • Your data never leaves your device
  • Fast, responsive, and distraction-free
  • Designed for late-night study sessions
